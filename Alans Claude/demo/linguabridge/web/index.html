    How we measured

    We wrote 12 everyday sentences, such as a doctor's instruction and a bus fare, in six languages. For each tokenizer we counted the tokens for all 12 and divided by the English count. The tokenizers are the real ones shipped by Meta and Mistral.

    What this does not show yet

    • Twelve sentences is a pilot. The full run uses the 1,012 professionally translated sentences of the FLORES+ dataset.
    • The sentences were translated with AI help. Each of us is checking our own language.
    • Token count is the cost. Whether answers also get worse, and whether the bridge fixes it, is measured separately on the Belebele reading test.
