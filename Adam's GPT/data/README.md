# Dataset decision (phase 1)
Selected executable dataset: `smoke.jsonl`, 48 newly AI-authored fictional DEVELOPMENT records, 8 each for English, Kurmanji, Hindi, Simplified Chinese, Spanish and Turkish. Provenance: generated by Codex GPT-6 Astra during this session. No private messages or contacts were collected. Names/places are explicit example inventions; emails use example.com/org and phone examples use the North American fictional 202-555-0100/0101 range. These are not localized phone coverage. No third-party corpus rows are redistributed. Human linguistic review is pending. Generated fixtures are project-authored; no claim is made that an external dataset license applies.

## External candidates checked
- REDACT: https://huggingface.co/datasets/guneeshv/REDACT-PII-Benchmark . Card provides character spans, 51 labels and 25 language variants including EN, HI, AR and Chinese variants, but no Kurmanji. Files are gated behind acceptance of REDACT Dataset Terms. Not downloaded; not used for results. Potential independent evaluation source for overlapping languages after access and label auditing.
- REDACT terms: https://huggingface.co/datasets/guneeshv/REDACT-PII-Benchmark/blob/main/LICENSE . Custom terms permit educational/defensive evaluation with attribution and redistribution conditions; this is not the software's MIT license. Do not bypass the gate. No gated terms were accepted in this phase.
- ai4privacy/pii-masking-300k: https://huggingface.co/datasets/ai4privacy/pii-masking-300k . Public preview exposes source text and labeled offsets, with six language classes and a custom license link. Full license and target-language suitability were not validated here; rejected for immediate ingestion. No rows copied into our dataset.

Decision: synthetic smoke data makes the software reproducible now; a research-grade benchmark remains pending. REDACT alone cannot support the requested Kurmanji comparison. Obtain independent licensed Kurmanji material or student-authored, fluent-speaker-reviewed fictional samples, separated by template families. Never present translated fixtures as natural-distribution performance.
