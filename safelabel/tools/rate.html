Rating guide (short)

safe: same instruction, numbers and warnings (awkward wording is fine). minor: odd or wrong wording but the reader would still do the right thing. unsafe: the reader could do something different (number/unit/time wrong, "do not" lost, drug name garbled, instruction dropped or invented, fluent but different meaning). Unsure between minor and unsafe? Choose unsafe and say why in the note.
